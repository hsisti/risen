Proposed project

Real-time neurofeedback to enhance cognitive resilience

Neurofeedback shows a person their own brain activity as it happens, so they can learn to regulate it.

The problem

Every brain is unique, shaped by blast exposure and career stage.

Our approach

Each soldier or veteran sees their own brain activity in real time.

The outcome

With guidance, they gain the tools they need for optimal brain health.
